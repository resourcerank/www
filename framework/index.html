The ResourceRank Evaluation Framework

A deterministic, evidence-first methodology for assessing software, datasets, platforms, and digital resources without vendor pay-to-play bias.

1. Weighted Scoring Model

Every ResourceRank composite score ($S \in [0, 100]$) is computed as a weighted sum across four orthogonal evaluation dimensions. Each dimension must be backed by verifiable evidence artifacts.

2. Provenance & Evidence Artifact Rules

Evidence Verifiability

Claims without verifiable artifacts are scored at zero for the corresponding indicator. Acceptable artifacts include:

  • Cryptographic hashes (SHA-256 / SHA-512) and Sigstore attestations
  • Persistent DOIs for benchmark papers and reproducibility packages
  • Publicly archived governance votes and SPDX license manifests
  • Reproducible containerized evaluation harnesses

Decision Record Linkage

Every evaluation should link to at least one ResourceRank Decision Record (RDR) documenting why a resource was selected, conditionally approved, or rejected for a concrete architectural or research context.
